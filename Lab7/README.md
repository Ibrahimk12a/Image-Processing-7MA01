
# Lab 7: Laplacian and Mathematical & Statistical Filters

In this lab, I did three main tasks using OpenCV:

### 1. Laplacian Filter
- Applied a 3x3 manual Laplacian kernel and the OpenCV Laplacian function to enhance edges in a color image.

### 2. Adding Noise
- Added Gaussian, Salt & Pepper, Exponential, and Uniform noise to a color image.

### 3. Mathematical and Statistical Filters
- Applied Arithmetic Mean, Geometric Mean, Harmonic Mean, Contra-Harmonic Mean, Median, Max, Min, and Midpoint filters to compare their effects on the image.
