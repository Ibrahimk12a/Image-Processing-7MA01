Image Processing: Lab 4

## What I Learned
In this lab, I learned how to manipulate image pixels directly to improve image quality and contrast. I explored different techniques to adjust brightness and separate objects from the background.

## What Was Accomplished
* **Histogram Stretching & Equalization:** Rescaled intensity values and flattened the histogram of an image to improve its overall contrast.
* **Histogram Matching:** Adjusted the color distribution of a source image to match the histogram of a reference image.
